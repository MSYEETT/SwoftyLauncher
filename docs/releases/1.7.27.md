# 1.7.27

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.7.27 fixes saved Grand Prix weekends that crashed while loading, stops asking for updates of addons that are off before Play, and gives addons fuel mixes they can tune, mid-season driver changes and dropped scores.

## Saves

- Continuing a saved Grand Prix weekend could crash at the garage cutscene after another addon took the car ids the weekend's cars had used. The launcher maps a saved weekend's old car ids to the new ones, but other addons' leftover entries for the same ids hid some of them, so those cars loaded with nothing in their place. The mapping now reads only the addons in the build, and an old id belongs to the car that held it.

## Play

- The launcher no longer asks you to update addons that are turned off before you can press Play.

## Addons

- Fuel mixes: an addon can read and change each car's Lean, Standard, Rich and Max fuel use, power, revs and engine temperature while it drives, and offer every mix on the MFD in the seasons it chooses. Rich Fuel Mix uses this.
- Mid-season driver changes: a career's season can list stand-ins for chosen rounds, and the launcher puts them in the seat at Team HQ before each round and brings the regular driver back afterwards. F1 1990 uses this for the real 1990 changes.
- Dropped scores: a career's drivers' championship can count only each driver's best results, as before 1991. F1 1990 counts the best 11 of 16.
