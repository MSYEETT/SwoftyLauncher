## Question
Which in-game actions re-read the VTF into an existing car's systems (Car_Assets_CreateSystemForPerformanceMode reached through Car_Systems_Reset_Thunk rather than Car_Chassis_Build)?

## Hypothesis
Car_Systems_Reset_Thunk (0x1447426c0) re-runs Car_Assets_CreateSystemForPerformanceMode on an existing car, so every VTF section is re-read into the existing systems through system vt+8 (Car_Read_<X>). Callers are Car_Reset_VehicleSystems (0x142f44c30) from three .data1 bodies: 0x148d925f0, the body of Game_Set_VehicleController_Thunk (0x14389cc90, controller change) and Car_Physics_ResettleVehicles_Body (0x1519ed8e0, teleport then a 1e-4 s settle step). Expected: flashback, reset-to-track, return-to-garage and handing the car to the AI (end of session / auto-drive into the pits) hit it; plain driving does not. The backtrace tells the create path (return into Car_Chassis_Build at 0x1446296ea) from the reset path (return into 0x1555bdf9b).
